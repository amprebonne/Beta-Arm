The robotic manipulator is a 5-DOF articulated arm composed of five revolute joints and corresponding links (\(a_{1}\)–\(a_{5}\)) that form its kinematic chain. Each joint provides a single rotational degree of freedom, enabling coordinated multi-axis motion suitable for basic pick-and-place tasks within a controlled workspace. A gripper end effector is mounted at the terminal link to handle lightweight objects, and the modular structure of the arm allows for easy mechanical adaptation and future extensions.

This directory contains all files related to the mechanical design of the Beta-Arm project. 
Mechanics focuses on the following:
- kinematics modeling & analysis
- mechanical CAD modeling with exports for 3-D printing
- updating the BOM - Bill of Materials with mechanical parts and expenses
